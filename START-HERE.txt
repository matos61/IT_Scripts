============================================================
 mac-migrate — move your dev setup to a new Mac in minutes
============================================================

Carries your tool configs + logins (gam, gcloud, gh, ssh, aws,
docker, rclone, git, …) to a new machine and verifies they work.
Everything is encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

------------------------------------------------------------
ON YOUR OLD MAC  (the one that already works)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. See what will transfer:
       ./mac-migrate.sh scan
   (Open manifest.txt and delete any line you DON'T want to move.)
3. Make the encrypted bundle:
       ./mac-migrate.sh bundle
   -> type a passphrase (remember it!). Produces:
       mac-migrate-bundle.tar.gz.enc

------------------------------------------------------------
MOVE IT
------------------------------------------------------------
Copy this WHOLE folder (scripts + the .enc bundle) to the new
Mac — AirDrop, USB stick, or scp. Keep the files together.

------------------------------------------------------------
ON YOUR NEW MAC
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run the installer:
       ./install-newmac.sh
   It finds the bundle, installs Homebrew + your tools, restores
   your configs, and runs a test suite. Enter the same passphrase
   when asked.
3. Open a NEW Terminal window when it finishes.

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING SHOWS [fail]
------------------------------------------------------------
Usually just needs a quick re-login, e.g.:
       gcloud auth login
       gh auth login
       gam oauth create
Then re-check:
       ./mac-migrate.sh test

------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------
• The .enc bundle holds real credentials. The passphrase is the
  only thing protecting it — pick a strong one, share it OUT OF
  BAND (not in the same email/chat as the file).
• Restore backs up anything it overwrites to ~/.mac-migrate-backup-*
• Full docs: README.md
